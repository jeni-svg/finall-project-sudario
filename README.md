# MindSet

A web-based personal study and quiz app where students write notes, create custom review questions, and take practice quizzes.

## Problem
• Students keep study notes and practice questions in separate places, making self-review and organization harder.
• Ready-made study apps make it hard to focus on the exact concepts and key details a student considers most important.

## Target Users
Students who want a personal space to organize notes, create review questions, and practice with quizzes.

## Solution
MindSet brings personal notes, user-created multiple-choice and identification questions, and random quiz practice together in one web app so students stay in control of their study materials.

## Main Feature
Students create and manage their own notes and questions to take interactive quizzes and review their results.

## Pages
• **Home Page**: Introduces MindSet with options to enter Guest Mode or view Login/Sign-Up.
• **Dashboard**: Main navigation hub to access Notes, Create Question / My Question, and Start Quiz.
• **Notes**: Free space to write, save, edit, and delete thoughts, reminders, ideas, or key study information.
• **Create Question / My Question**: Create multiple-choice or identification questions, view, edit, or delete saved questions, and launch a quiz.
• **Quiz Mode (Random)**: Displays saved questions one at a time in random order with controls to move to the next question or exit.
• **Quiz Result**: Shows the final score, correct and wrong counts, selected answers, and correct answers, with options to try again or return to the dashboard.
